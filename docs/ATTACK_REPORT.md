# Attack report (internal round)

One round of attacks against BackedBy's own code, run after the offline suite passed and before anything was
deployed for real, plus a second round (H1 revised, H4, M5) after review of the first seeds. Every finding is written
as an attack in `test/test_attacks.py` or `test/test_vaults.py` that now fails, and every High and Medium is fixed in
commit **`91526ea`** (first round) or **`bf24598`** (second round) as the table says.

Scope of the round: unauthenticated pair tricks, block and timestamp games, decimals, proxy tokens, rebasing tokens,
re-entrancy of payouts, contest abuse, griefing, and whether the docs say what the code does.

| H1 | High | **Lookalike OP Stack token.** Deploy a contract on Optimism or Base that answers `remoteToken() = USDC` and `bridge() = L2StandardBridge`, mint 10^30 of it outside the bridge, and add it as a destination. | First fix (`0f4bf9b`): count `min(totalSupply, deposits)`. | M3 | Medium | **In-flight deposit flash.** Tokens locked on Ethereum but not yet minted on the destination count as locked and not as supply (that is the rule that keeps in-flight transfers from ever looking under-backed). ## Also checked, nothing found

- **Fake vaults.** An attacker's adapter whose `token()` is USDT and whose OFT, peers and Polygon token are the
  attacker's own authenticates (it is a real OFT setup), but only against the attacker's token, so `add_vault` and
  `add_destination` refuse it for the USDT pair; pointing a fake adapter's peer at the real Polygon OFT does not help,
  because the adapter is derived from the real OFT's `peers(Ethereum)`.

- **Fake pairs.** A plain ERC-20, an EOA, Circle's native USDC on OP passed off as bridged USDC, a token on the wrong
  chain for its bridge kind, a Nomad representation from another domain, a source token Nomad does not hold as local:
  all refused at authentication (`test_reader.py`), and on Studio Dev with real chain data (native USDC on OP,
  `REMOTE_TOKEN_IS_NOT_THE_SOURCE_TOKEN`).
- **Omitted destinations.** An operator registers only one of two Standard Bridge representations; anyone adds the
  other with the same proof and the next check counts it (`Destinations.test_omitted_destination_cannot_hide_supply`).
- **Timestamp games.** Destination newer than source by one second, gap of exactly 600 s (rules) and 601 s
  (INCONCLUSIVE), Polygon 300/301 s, swapped block numbers, blocks above either RPC's finalized block: all decided by
  code, with the stake refunded where the filing was at fault.
- **In-flight transfers inside the gap** only ever over-back (`BlockGames`), as RULES.md argues.
- **Forged leaders.** A leader that alters a vault, a decimals value, a balance or claims an INCONCLUSIVE it did not
  observe is outvoted (`test_reader.py`, `FORGE`).
- **Payables.** None of the four payable methods contains a `raise` (AST test); garbage arguments refund.
- **Stuck states.** Every non-terminal state reaches FINAL or INCONCLUSIVE through a permissionless call after its
  deadline, including with every RPC down forever (`StuckStates`).
- **Conservation.** A 160-step random sequence of every operation keeps `received = bonds + open + claimable +
  claimed` after every step and drains to zero (`Conservation`).
